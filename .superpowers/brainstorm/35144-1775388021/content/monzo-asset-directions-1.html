Monzo Export Asset Directions

We are choosing the source-asset system for the Figma/plugin export itself, not the storefront page.

Current failure mode: the pipeline treats raw screenshots as final merchandising assets. That gives us a technically valid kit, but not a premium exported artifact. The strongest fix is to add a dedicated cover/preview composition layer before packaging.

Risk: premium positioning keeps collapsing at export time

Turn selected screens into consistent framed mockups with guided crops, background art, and a polished thumbnail set.

Build: screenshot ranking + collage templates
Output: clearly better, still screenshot-led

Good midpoint if we want a fast commercial uplift without designing a full export identity.
